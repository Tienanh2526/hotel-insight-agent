# Research Hypotheses — Unvalidated

## H1
Some target users spend meaningful time summarizing online reviews.
Evidence needed: recent example, frequency, time spent, current workaround.

## H2
Users need help prioritizing repeated themes versus severe but rare issues.
Evidence needed: observe how they currently choose what to fix and compare prototype output.

## H3
Evidence-backed findings are more useful than generic AI summaries.
Evidence needed: user verifies source quotes and marks findings actionable/not actionable.

## H4
Existing OTA dashboards, reputation tools, PMS, spreadsheets, or manual workflows may already solve the problem.
Evidence needed: inventory current tools and identify residual gaps.

Do not call a hypothesis validated because people say an idea sounds interesting. Look for past behavior and realistic prototype use.
